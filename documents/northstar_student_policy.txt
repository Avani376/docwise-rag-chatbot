NORTHSTAR UNIVERSITY — STUDENT SERVICES AND ACADEMIC POLICIES
Demonstration document. Northstar University is fictional; these policies are sample content only.

ACADEMIC CALENDAR
The fall semester begins September 3. The last day to add a course is September 12. The fall reading period begins December 9. Final examinations run from December 12 through December 20. The spring semester begins January 21.

COURSE REGISTRATION
Students may add a course through September 12 in the fall term. To withdraw from a course without academic penalty, submit the online withdrawal form by October 31. A withdrawn course appears as W on the transcript and does not affect the grade point average. Students should contact their academic adviser before withdrawing.

ATTENDANCE
Students are expected to attend all scheduled class meetings. An absence is excused for documented illness, a religious observance, or a university-sponsored activity. Send documentation to the instructor within five business days after returning. Three or more unexcused absences may lower the participation grade. Instructors must publish their course-specific attendance policy in the syllabus.

EXTENSIONS AND LATE WORK
Students who need an assignment extension should contact the instructor before the deadline. Instructors may grant an extension of up to seven calendar days. Work submitted late without an approved extension loses five percent of its points per calendar day, for up to five days; after five days the instructor may assign zero. Disability-related accommodations follow the Accessibility Services process and are not limited by this general extension rule.

LIBRARY
The main library is open Monday through Thursday from 7 a.m. to midnight, Friday from 7 a.m. to 8 p.m., and Saturday and Sunday from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. The library is closed on university holidays. Students may borrow up to 20 books at a time. The standard loan period is 28 days. One renewal is allowed if no other borrower has placed a hold.

FINANCIAL AID
Students must submit the annual aid renewal form by April 15. The financial aid office sends award notices to the student's university email by June 30. Students whose household income changes by at least 20 percent may request a review by submitting a special-circumstances form. Work-study positions are limited to 15 hours per week during the academic term.

STUDENT HOUSING
Residence hall applications for the fall term are due May 1. A $200 housing deposit is required when submitting the application. Room assignments are sent by July 15. Quiet hours are 10 p.m. to 8 a.m. Sunday through Thursday, and midnight to 9 a.m. Friday and Saturday. Overnight guests may stay no more than three consecutive nights and must be registered with the residence hall desk.

ACADEMIC INTEGRITY
Submitting another person's work as one's own, unauthorized collaboration, and using prohibited materials during an assessment are academic misconduct. Instructors report suspected cases to the Academic Integrity Office within ten business days. A student may submit a written response within five business days of receiving notice. Sanctions range from a warning to suspension, depending on severity and prior findings. Students may appeal a finding within ten business days.

ACCESSIBILITY SERVICES
Students seeking academic accommodations should register with Accessibility Services and provide supporting documentation. The office reviews complete requests within ten business days. Approved accommodation letters are sent to the student and instructors. Accommodations apply prospectively after instructors receive the letter; they do not automatically change grades or deadlines from prior terms.

HEALTH AND WELLNESS
The campus health center is open Monday through Friday from 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. Appointments can be scheduled through the student portal or by phone. The center offers primary care and routine vaccinations. For emergencies outside opening hours, call local emergency services. Counseling Services offers up to eight individual sessions per academic year at no charge.

STUDENT CLUBS
New clubs must have at least eight currently enrolled student members and a faculty or staff adviser. Applications are submitted to the Student Activities Office by October 1 for fall recognition. Recognized clubs receive an annual programming budget of up to $500. Funding requests above that amount require approval from the Student Activities Committee.

TECHNOLOGY
Campus Wi-Fi is available in academic and residential buildings. Students can connect using their university account. The IT Help Desk is open Monday through Friday from 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. Loaner laptops may be borrowed for up to seven days, subject to availability. The university recommends enabling multi-factor authentication on all accounts.
